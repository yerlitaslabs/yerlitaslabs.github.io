Privacy Policy

Effective: 14 October 2026

1. Summary

BarcodeVault stores everything you record — counts, barcode values, quantities, locations, notes, the codes you generate and backups — only on your device. The developer does not receive this data, and the app has no user accounts and no analytics of its own. The app is free of charge and shows ads from Google AdMob; for this, and for barcode recognition diagnostics, Google receives certain technical data from your device, as described in "Advertising and Google services". Information reaches the developer only if you decide to contact us yourself (see "Contacting us").

2. Data that stays on your device

Everything you create in the app — counts, barcode values, quantities, locations, notes, the codes you generate, settings and backups — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Camera images are analysed in real time in the device's memory to recognise barcodes; they are never saved, recorded or transmitted.

3. Advertising and Google services

The app shows two kinds of ads: optional rewarded video ads, which you can choose to watch to unlock starting a new count, scanning an image, creating a QR code or creating a barcode for 1 hour, and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads in Settings. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland; Google LLC elsewhere), which acts as an independent controller. To deliver ads, measure them, limit how often they appear and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived), device and app information (such as model, operating system, language and app version) and information about the ads shown and your interaction with them. Where the law requires it — in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and certain other regions — Google's consent form is shown and ads are personalised only with your consent; you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy options. In Turkey, the app requests only non-personalised ads; this is determined from the country of your SIM card or mobile network, your time zone or your device region. Non-personalised ads are based on context (such as the app and the approximate location) rather than on your profile, although Google may still use the data above for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention. Elsewhere you can limit personalised ads by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in Android settings. Your counts, barcode values, notes, photos and documents are never passed to the ad SDK. Google ML Kit recognises barcodes on the device and does not send camera images or barcode contents; it may send Google technical diagnostics (device model, Android version, app version, a per-installation identifier, performance and error data). The barcode reader zxing-cpp and the code generator ZXing work entirely offline, and Android's automatic cloud backup is disabled for this app. Google may process this data in other countries, including the United States. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. The app is intended for adults and business use and is not directed at children.

The developer has no access to the data you record and therefore does not act as a controller or processor of it. You — or the organisation on whose behalf you use the app — are solely responsible for that data within the meaning of applicable data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and comparable laws. If you record information that identifies other people (for example names in notes or serial numbers assigned to individuals), it is your responsibility to have a lawful basis, to inform those people and to respect their rights. For the data processed by Google to show ads and for ML Kit diagnostics, Google acts as an independent controller under its own privacy policy; the developer only receives aggregated reports that do not identify you (for example, the number of ads shown).

5. Permissions

Camera: used only while the scanner is open, to read barcodes. Notifications (optional): after you accept these terms, Android asks whether to allow notifications; if you allow them, reminders, summaries, tips and backup alerts are turned on, and each type can be turned off in Settings › Notifications. Vibration: used for scan feedback. Photos and files: the app does not request access to your photo library or storage; when you choose a photo or document, Android's system picker gives the app temporary access to that single file only. Permissions are granted, and can be withdrawn at any time, only by you in Android settings. Withdrawing a permission disables only the related feature; all other features keep working. Location: the app does not request or use your location. Time-based features, such as the greeting on the home screen, use only your device's clock and time-zone setting. Internet and network state: used only to load ads and the consent form and for the Google services described in "Advertising and Google services". Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described there; you can delete or reset it in Android settings at any time.

6. Photos and documents you scan

When you use "Scan image", the photo or PDF you select is read and analysed only in the device's memory to find codes. The file is not copied, uploaded or kept by the app, and the preview is discarded when you leave the screen. Codes that you choose to add to a count are stored like any other record. You can also send a photo or PDF to BarcodeVault from another app with Android's Share menu; the app then receives temporary access to that single file only and handles it in the same way.

7. Exports, sharing and printing

The app never sends your records or files by itself. An export, backup or code file is created only when you request it and is delivered only to the destination you choose (for example the Android share menu, an e-mail app, cloud storage or a folder). Printing uses the Android print service you select; that service, which may be provided by your printer manufacturer or another company, handles the document under its own terms. Once you share or print a file, it is handled under the terms of the recipient or service you chose, and you are responsible for that transfer.

8. Backups and security

Backups are stored privately in the app on your device and are kept up to the number you choose. You can optionally encrypt backups with AES-256 using your own password; the password is protected by your device's secure key store and is never known to the developer. A forgotten password cannot be recovered by anyone. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep copies of important backups in a safe place.

9. Contacting us

If you send us feedback or a question by email, your own email app sends the message directly to the developer. We then receive your email address, the content of your message and any technical details you choose to leave in it (app version, Android version and device model are pre-filled and can be deleted before sending). We use this information only to answer you and to improve the app, we do not share it with third parties, and we delete it when it is no longer needed for these purposes. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; Art. 5(2)(f) KVKK). Our email service is provided by Google (Gmail), so correspondence may be stored on servers outside your country, including in the United States; this transfer takes place because you choose to email us and is necessary to provide the email service. Under applicable data protection law you have the right to request access to, correction or erasure of your correspondence and to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority of your country.

10. Retention, deletion and your rights

Your data remains on your device until you delete it (individual records, counts or backups) or uninstall the app, which removes all app data from the device. You can access and take your data with you at any time using export or backup, and erase it using the delete functions. Because the developer holds no data about you from the app, requests to access, correct or erase that data cannot be fulfilled by the developer — you can do this directly in the app. Requests about correspondence you have sent us are handled as described in "Contacting us". Data that Google collects for ads is kept and handled under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy); you can exercise your rights regarding that data with Google, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time.

Oluşturduğunuz QR kodların ve barkodların içeriğinden ve bunları nerede kullandığınızdan siz sorumlusunuz. Zararlı, yanıltıcı veya hukuka aykırı içeriğe yönlendiren kodlar oluşturmayın; Wi-Fi parolası veya kişisel veri içeren kodları koruyun. Perakendede satılan ürünlerde kullanılan EAN, UPC ve diğer GS1 tanımlama numaralarının GS1 tarafından tahsis edilmesi veya marka sahibince yetkilendirilmesi gerekir; uygulama yalnızca girdiğiniz numarayı çizer, numara tahsis etmez veya tescil etmez. Basılı kodları kullanmadan önce deneyin.

6. Veri kaybı ve yedekler

Tüm veriler yalnızca cihazınızda saklanır. Yedek almak ve saklamak sizin sorumluluğunuzdadır. Uygulamayı kaldırmak veya cihazı kaybetmek uygulamada saklanan verileri siler. Şifreli yedekler parolası olmadan açılamaz.

7. Garanti verilmemesi

Uygulama, yürürlükteki hukukun izin verdiği ölçüde, belirli bir amaca uygunluk ve kesintisiz veya hatasız çalışma dahil açık veya zımni hiçbir garanti verilmeksizin "olduğu gibi" ve "mevcut haliyle" sunulur.

8. Sorumluluğun sınırlandırılması

Yürürlükteki hukukun izin verdiği azami ölçüde geliştirici; uygulamanın kullanımından veya kullanılamamasından doğan dolaylı, arızi veya sonuç niteliğindeki zararlardan, veri kaybından, kâr kaybından veya iş kesintisinden sorumlu değildir. Bu koşulların hiçbir hükmü, kast veya ağır ihmal gibi yasa gereği sınırlandırılamayan sorumluluğu sınırlamaz.

9. Tüketici hakları ve uygulanacak hukuk

Bu koşullar, ikamet ettiğiniz ülkenin mevzuatı uyarınca tüketici olarak sahip olduğunuz emredici hakları etkilemez.

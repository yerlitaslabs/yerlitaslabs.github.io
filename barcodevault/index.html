BarcodeVault

Inventory barcode scanner for Android – offline stock counting

BarcodeVault is a free inventory app and barcode scanner for Android. It turns your phone into a fast stock counter for warehouses, stores, stockrooms and home inventories: scan, count and export your stocktake to Excel, CSV or PDF. Counting works offline, and your records never leave your device.

Made for

  • Shops and retail – stock counts, stocktaking and checking deliveries
  • Warehouses and stockrooms – inventory by location, aisle and shelf
  • Offices and IT – asset tracking with serial numbers (duplicates are rejected with a warning)
  • Small businesses – online sellers, cafés, pharmacies, libraries and home collections

Scan & count

Continuous scanning with sound and vibration. Choose what happens with repeated codes: add to the quantity, warn, or add a separate row. Tap Finish to go straight to saving or sharing.

Excel, CSV & PDF

Export any count to Excel (.xlsx), CSV or a print-ready PDF report in one tap. Leading zeros are kept.

QR codes & barcodes

Create QR codes for links, Wi-Fi and contacts, or EAN, UPC and Code 128 barcodes. Save as PNG, JPG, PDF or SVG, or print.

Photos & PDFs

Read every barcode and QR code in a photo from your gallery or in a PDF document, and add them to a count.

Backups & reminders

Automatic daily or weekly backups, optionally encrypted with AES-256 and your own password. Optional reminders about your own counts.

Private by design

No account. Counts, notes, photos and backups stay on your device. Free, supported by ads.

How to count inventory with your phone

  1. Start a count and give it a name, for example “Store 1 – November”.
  2. Scan the barcodes with the camera. Each scan adds 1 to the quantity; type a number for boxes, add a location or a note.
  3. Tap Finish and export the count to Excel, CSV or PDF – save it or send it to your accountant or ERP.

Free tools for bigger inventories

No subscription: every tool is free. Like QR code and barcode creation, each one opens for 1 hour after a short video.

  • Scan photos & PDFs – read every barcode and QR code in a photo or PDF and add them to a count.
  • Combined Excel – export several counts into one workbook, with totals per barcode across all counts.
  • Search all counts – type or scan a barcode and see in which counts it appears, with quantities and locations.
  • Compare with expected stock – import your stock list (Excel or CSV) and see missing, extra and different quantities.
  • Batch label printing – print many barcodes or QR codes on A4 label sheets as a PDF.

Continuing an existing count, exporting and backups are always free and never need a video.

Frequently asked questions

Is BarcodeVault free?

Yes. BarcodeVault is free and supported by ads, with no subscription. New counts, image scanning, QR and barcode creation and the tools open for 1 hour after a short video. Continuing a count, exporting and backups are always free.

Does BarcodeVault work offline?

Yes. Scanning, counting, exporting and backups work without an internet connection. Barcode recognition runs on the phone; the internet is only used for ads.

Which barcode types can it read?

22 types, including QR Code, Micro QR, rMQR, Data Matrix, PDF417, Aztec, MaxiCode, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, ITF, Codabar and the GS1 DataBar family.

Can I export my inventory count to Excel?

Yes. Every count can be exported to Excel (.xlsx), CSV or a printable PDF report, saved to your phone or shared. Leading zeros are kept and identical barcodes can be merged into one row with total quantities.

Where are my counts stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud upload. Optional backup files can be encrypted with AES-256 and your own password.

Can I compare a count with my stock list?

Yes. Import your expected stock list as Excel or CSV and BarcodeVault shows missing items, extra items and different quantities.

Is BarcodeVault available for iPhone?

No. BarcodeVault is an Android app for phones with Android 8.0 or later, available on Google Play.

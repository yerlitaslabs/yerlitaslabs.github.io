BarcodeVault

Offline barcode inventory counting for Android

Free on Google Play

BarcodeVault turns your phone into a fast barcode counter for warehouses, stores, stockrooms and home inventories. Scan, count and export – counting works offline, and your records never leave your device.

Scan & count

Continuous scanning with sound and vibration. Choose what happens with repeated codes: add to the quantity, warn, or add a separate row.

Photos & PDFs

Read barcodes and QR codes from photos in your gallery or from PDF documents.

QR codes & barcodes

Create QR codes for links, Wi-Fi and contacts, or EAN, UPC and Code 128 barcodes. Save as PNG, JPG, PDF or SVG, or print.

Excel & CSV

Export any count to Excel (.xlsx) or CSV in one tap. Leading zeros are kept.

Backups

Automatic daily or weekly backups, optionally encrypted with AES-256 and your own password.

Private by design

No account. Counts, notes, photos and backups stay on your device. Free with optional short video ads.

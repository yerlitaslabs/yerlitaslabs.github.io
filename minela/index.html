Questions

What kind of game is Minela?

A block puzzle on an 8×8 board. Drag three pieces at a time onto the board; full rows and columns clear. Clear on move after move to build a combo, all the way to MINELA at ×10.

How many levels are there?

5,000 Adventure levels in 250 chapters, each named after a famous mosaic site such as Zeugma, Ephesus, Isfahan and Kyoto. There is also a Daily Puzzle every day and an endless Classic mode.

Is it free? Are there ads?

Minela is free and has nothing to buy. There are no banners: a short ad shows now and then between games, and rewarded videos play only when you choose them.

Does it need the internet?

Yes, Minela plays online, with up to 15 minutes of offline play a day.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Sign in with Google Play Games to keep your progress in the cloud, and compete on two leaderboards.

Which languages?

English, Turkish, German, French and Spanish.

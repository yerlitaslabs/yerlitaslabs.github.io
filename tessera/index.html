Questions

What kind of game is Tessera?

A logic puzzle about light: you rotate mirrors and place prisms so that every beam reaches the socket of its own color. It is calm (no timer, no lives) but real: the later tiers ask for long chains of reflections, split beams and color fusions.

Is it free? Does it have ads?

It is free, with no in-app purchases. There are no banners; a short full-screen ad may show now and then between screens (never during a puzzle, at most one every 90 seconds), and you can choose to watch short videos for an extra hint or a free booster.

How many levels are there?

1,000, in ten tiers from a single mirror to optical matrices of fused colors. Every tenth level is a gold-framed Keystone, the hardest of its chapter.

Can I play offline?

Yes, the puzzles play without an internet connection. Ads and Play Games achievements need a connection.

Which devices does it run on?

Android 8.0 or later, on phones, tablets and foldables (large screens turn freely).

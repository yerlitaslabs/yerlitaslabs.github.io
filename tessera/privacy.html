Privacy Policy

Tessera: The Light Weaver · Effective date: 6 November 2026

In short: Tessera itself collects no data. The offline edition has no ads, no account and no analytics, and does not even request the internet permission. Your progress stays on your device. The edition with Google Play Games and ads is described in section 8; section 9 tells you how to delete your data.

1. Who we are

Tessera ("the app") is a puzzle game for Android published by YerlitAS Labs ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, write to yerlitaslabs@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The app keeps a small file on your phone with the levels you solved, your fewest turns per level, the stars, Lumen, Essence and Atelier unlocks you earned, and your settings (sound, vibration, language, look). It never leaves the device: it is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is excluded from Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it (see section 9).

3. Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data, device identifiers, location, usage statistics or crash reports. The offline edition contains no third-party SDKs for advertising or analytics; the edition on Google Play contains Google's, and what they process is described in section 8.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration: for the gentle haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Notifications (asked once, on Android 13 and later; you may say no): a daily reminder, at most once a day and only on a day you have not played, that your next window is waiting. It is made on the phone itself; nothing is sent. Turn it off any time in Settings › Notifications.
  • Run at startup: so the reminder's alarm survives a restart of the phone. It does nothing else.
  • No internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions are requested.

5. E-mails to us

If you write to us (the address is above and on the game's Google Play page), we receive only what you decide to send and use it solely to answer you. You may ask us to delete our correspondence at any time.

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date before the version of the app it concerns is released.

8. The edition with Google Play Games and ads

Tessera exists in two editions. Everything above describes the offline edition. If the app's Google Play page says it contains ads, you have the edition with Google services, and the following applies to it as well:

  • Google Play Games (achievements): if you are signed in to Play Games, Google records the achievements you unlock under your Play Games profile. You can open them or sign in from Settings › Achievements; signing in is optional and the game plays the same without it. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we see no personal data.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): now and then a short full-screen ad between screens (after a few levels, on opening a constellation; never more than one in two minutes), and videos you choose to watch for a hint or a booster. No banners. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may process your device's advertising ID, IP address and diagnostic data. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and where US state laws require it, a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice first; you can change it any time in Settings › Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences. See how Google uses data from partner apps.
  • This edition requests the internet permission, used only by these Google services. The game itself still sends nothing to us.

9. Deleting your data

Tessera has no account and keeps no copy of anything on a server, so you can delete everything yourself:

  • Your progress (solved levels, stars, Lumen, Essence, Atelier unlocks and settings): Android Settings › Apps › Tessera › Storage › Clear storage erases it at once, and so does uninstalling the app. No copy is kept anywhere else.
  • Your e-mails to us: write to yerlitaslabs@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We delete our correspondence with you within 30 days and keep nothing.
  • Advertising data (edition with ads): change your consent in Tessera's Settings › Privacy choices, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (on most phones Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads). The data Google AdMob processes is kept and deleted by Google as described in how Google retains data.
  • Play Games achievements (edition with Play Games): in the Google Play Games app, open Settings and choose to delete your Play Games account and data. Google then deletes your Play Games profile and the achievements in it.

  • Ejecución al iniciar: para que la alarma del recordatorio sobreviva a un reinicio del teléfono. No hace nada más.

Sin banners. Para mostrar y medir los anuncios, Google AdMob puede tratar el ID de publicidad de tu dispositivo, tu dirección IP y datos de diagnóstico. En el Espacio Económico Europeo, el Reino Unido y Suiza, y donde lo exijan las leyes de los estados de EE. UU., un formulario de consentimiento (User Messaging Platform de Google) te pide primero tu elección; puedes cambiarla en cualquier momento en Ajustes › Opciones de privacidad. También puedes restablecer o eliminar tu ID de publicidad en los ajustes de tu teléfono. Los anuncios están limitados a contenido clasificado para todos los públicos. Consulta cómo usa Google los datos de las aplicaciones asociadas.

9. Eliminar tus datos

Tessera no tiene cuenta y no guarda ninguna copia en un servidor, así que puedes eliminarlo todo tú mismo:

  • Tu progreso (niveles resueltos, estrellas, Lumen, Esencia cromática, desbloqueos del Taller y ajustes): Ajustes de Android › Aplicaciones › Tessera › Almacenamiento › Borrar almacenamiento lo elimina al instante, igual que desinstalar la aplicación. No se guarda ninguna copia en otro lugar.
  • Tus correos: escribe a yerlitaslabs@gmail.com con el asunto «Eliminar mis datos». Eliminamos nuestra correspondencia contigo en un plazo de 30 días y no conservamos nada.
  • Datos publicitarios (edición con anuncios): cambia tu consentimiento en Ajustes › Opciones de privacidad de Tessera, y restablece o elimina tu ID de publicidad en los ajustes de tu teléfono (en la mayoría de los teléfonos, Ajustes › Google › Anuncios, o Ajustes › Privacidad › Anuncios). Google conserva y elimina los datos que trata Google AdMob como se describe en cómo conserva Google los datos.
  • Logros de Play Games (edición con Play Games): en la aplicación Google Play Games, abre Ajustes y elige eliminar tu cuenta y tus datos de Play Games. Google eliminará entonces tu perfil de Play Games y los logros que contiene.
